# Feature list (for the Store's "App features": up to 20 lines of 200 characters)

- Works fully offline: your Bibles, books and notes are on your computer
- Read several Bibles side by side, with cross-references and word look-up
- Fast search across every Bible and book in your library
- Full editor for notes and sermons, with headings, lists, tables and credited quotations
- Highlights in five colours, shown beside the passage
- Folders, tags and lists to keep your work in order
- Reading plans with progress
- Add your own books and PDFs, or download free Bibles from open catalogues
- Print and export with the right credit lines and quoting limits
- Optional AI assistant that reads your library and suggests drafts (off until you choose a provider)
- Backups and exports in open formats: your data is never locked in
- No accounts, no tracking, no usage statistics
- Free and open source, MIT or Apache-2.0
